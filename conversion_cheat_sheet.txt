LENGTH CONVERSIONS FOR PART ORDERING:
  Common M3 screw lengths: 10mm, 15mm, 20mm, 25mm
  NEMA 17 stepper shaft diameter: 4 mm = 0.16 inches
  
  Quick conversions (multiply by):
    mm → inches: ÷ 25.4 or × 0.03937
    cm → inches: ÷ 2.54 or × 0.3937  
    feet → meters: × 0.3048

MASS CONVERSIONS FOR SHIPPING ESTIMATES:
  g → kg: ÷ 1000
  g → pounds: ÷ 453.6 or × 0.002205
  g → ounces: ÷ 28.35 or × 0.03527

TEMPERATURE CONVERSIONS (IMU Sensor Calibration):
  °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
  °C = (°F - 32) × 5/9
  
  BMI088 operating range: -40°C to +85°C (-40°F to +185°F)

GEAR RATIO CALCULATIONS (Motor Selection):
  Ratio = driven_teeth / driver_teeth
  Example: 60-tooth gear driven by 20-tooth pinion = 3.0:1 reduction
  
  Wheel velocity formula: v = r × ω
    where r = wheel radius, ω = angular velocity in rad/s

BATTERY CAPACITY CONVERSIONS:
  mAh → Wh: (mAh ÷ 1000) × voltage
  Example: 2000mAh @ 7.4V = 14.8 Wh
  
  Wh → mAh: (Wh ÷ voltage) × 1000
